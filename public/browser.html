Choose a route and open a local Firefox tab.
1 · Select

Choose a region or node

NekoRoute creates a short-lived one-time route ticket. The raw public proxy address is handed only to the installed extension.

2 · Browse

Firefox opens a normal tab

HTML, JavaScript, cookies, media and normal navigation run in your local Firefox rather than inside a server-side iframe.

3 · Download

Files save directly to your PC

Downloads travel from the selected proxy to your Firefox. NekoRoute does not spool the file to its VPS in this mode.

Important: this mode uses a local Firefox extension because a web page is not allowed to silently change your browser's proxy configuration. The selected public proxy can see your connection and is not trusted infrastructure. Do not send passwords, private tokens, payment information or sensitive cookies through random public proxies.
